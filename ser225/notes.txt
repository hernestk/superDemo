Waterfall SDLC (Software Development Life Cycle)
Benefits
    - You do one phase and can move on until you complete the phase you're on.
    - Simple since it is linear
Cons
    - An issue can halt the production of the software being developed
    - Pretty rigid as it is linear
    - Customer is not involved and cannot change requirements once process has started

Agile SDLC
Benefits
    - Current industry standard
    - Iterative
    - Smaller repeating work cycles
    - Continously deliver value to customer
    - Customer can change requirements if necessary
        - pivot
        - adapt
        - fast failure
    - Customers can see incremental progress rather
    - Planning, working, and testing in smaller increments is more manageable
    - Risk is mitigated as time goes on
Cons
    - Customer not being involved can hurt the process
    - A weak link in an agile team can hurt process immensly
    - Not particularly "speedy"
    - Expectations are not solidified from onset of project (timeline, scope, cost)
